Preparing your project

What helps an edit run smoothly

A clear brief saves revision time. Send the raw footage, the intended audience, where the finished video will appear and examples of the pace or visual style you like. If there are brand fonts, colors, logos or music requirements, include those from the start.

Useful details to share

  • Target length and the platforms or aspect ratios you need.
  • Footage format, number of cameras and audio sources.
  • Must-keep moments, captions, graphics or calls to action.
  • Whether you need a full episode, short clips or both.
  • Review contact, delivery date and export requirements.

Finding the right format

One story, several useful edits

Long-form episode

For a podcast or documentary, the edit needs a clear opening, a sensible structure and enough breathing room for the story. Multi-camera changes should help the viewer follow the conversation. Dialogue cleanup, sound balance and color consistency make the finished episode easier to watch.

Short social clips

A strong short clip has one idea. We identify a moment that works on its own, set context quickly and adapt the frame for the platform. Captions, cutaways and graphics can help comprehension, but they should not distract from what the speaker or product is saying.

Brand and product video

A brand edit should make the intended action or message clear. We can combine supplied footage with title design, motion graphics, sound and color work. The pace and visual treatment follow the audience and placement rather than a fixed template.

Delivery

What an editing handoff can include

Before editing starts, we agree on the number of videos, lengths, formats and review steps. That keeps the final exports useful across the channels you actually plan to publish on.

  • A master edit in the agreed resolution and aspect ratio.
  • Platform versions for horizontal or vertical viewing when scoped.
  • Captions, titles and branded graphic assets where needed.
  • Balanced audio and consistent color across the finished cut.
  • Export files named clearly for review and publishing.

Common questions

Video editing FAQs

Can one recording become multiple formats?

Yes. A long conversation can be edited as a full episode and adapted into shorter vertical clips. We define the clips and format requirements in the brief.

Do you edit footage recorded elsewhere?

Yes. We can work from supplied footage and audio. Reviewing the source files first helps us identify any technical limits or missing material.

Can motion graphics be added to an existing edit?

Yes. Titles, lower thirds, diagrams, branded transitions and overlays can be scoped as a separate motion design task or included in the edit.
